# Historical stimulus-construction protocol

The accompanying file, **AI prompt and Human coding rubric.docx**, is preserved as a historical record of the stimulus-construction workflow.

It contains:
- the original clause-level AI scoring prompt for scapegoating rhetoric;
- the original 0–3 human coding rubric; and
- the historical rationale used when constructing high-contrast stimulus sets.

## Interpretation warning

This document predates the revised manuscript terminology and contains legacy phrases such as “scapegoating misinformation” and “true information.” Those labels are **not** the current construct definition and should not be treated as factual truth/falsity validation.

The AI scoring rule and the human rubric also use different scales and thresholds. The document does not, by itself, establish individual coder ratings, inter-rater reliability, adjudication records, or factual veracity of the posts.

Its purpose here is provenance and transparency, not current inferential validation.
